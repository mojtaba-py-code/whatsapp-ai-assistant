# Payments and insurance

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, debit and credit cards, and bank transfer. Credit card payments can be split into up to 3 interest-free installments.

## Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we work with several Argentine health insurance plans (obras sociales and prepagas). Coverage depends on each plan and the team confirms it before the appointment. Bring your insurance card and your ID on the day of the appointment.

## Do you issue invoices?

Yes, we issue an invoice (factura) for every payment.
