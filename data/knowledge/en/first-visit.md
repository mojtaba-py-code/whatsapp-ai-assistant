# First visit

## What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring your ID, your insurance card if you have one, and any previous X-rays or test results if you have them.

## What happens at the first visit?

The first visit takes about 30 minutes. The dentist examines your mouth, explains what they find and proposes a treatment plan with a quote. If an X-ray is needed, it is taken during the visit.

## Minors

Patients under 18 must come with a responsible adult.
