# Opening hours

## What days and hours are you open?

- Monday to Friday: 09:00 to 19:00.
- Saturday: 09:00 to 13:00.
- Sunday and public holidays: closed.

All times are Buenos Aires time (Argentina, UTC-3).

## Do you answer on WhatsApp outside opening hours?

The virtual assistant answers on WhatsApp 24 hours a day. People from the team reply during opening hours; outside those hours, they reply on the next business day.
