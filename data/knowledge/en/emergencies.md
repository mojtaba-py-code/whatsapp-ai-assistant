# Emergencies

## Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. During opening hours we keep slots for dental emergencies: severe pain, a broken tooth, an infection or a blow to the mouth. Type "human" in this chat and the team will give you the first free slot.

## What should I do in an emergency outside opening hours?

Outside opening hours there is no on-call service at the clinic. In a serious emergency, such as bleeding that does not stop, a swollen face with fever or a hard blow to the face, go to a hospital emergency room.

## In the meantime

For pain, you can apply a cold pack to the outside of your face. Do not take medication without professional advice.
