# Children's care

## From what age do you see children?

We see children from their first year of life. We recommend the first visit when the first teeth come in.

## What are children's visits like?

Children's dentistry visits take about 30 minutes and use games and simple explanations so the child feels comfortable. A responsible adult always accompanies the child.

## Preventing cavities in children

We offer sealants and fluoride treatments to prevent cavities. We recommend a check-up every 6 months.
