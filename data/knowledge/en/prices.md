# Prices and services

Clínica Dental Sonrisa is a fictional demo business: every price is an example.

## How much does each service cost?

- General check-up: ARS 30,000.
- Dental cleaning (prophylaxis): ARS 45,000.
- In-office teeth whitening: ARS 180,000.
- Cavity filling (simple filling): from ARS 55,000.
- Orthodontics consultation: ARS 35,000.
- Children's dentistry consultation: ARS 28,000.

## What is included in the price?

Prices include the appointment and basic materials. X-rays are charged separately: ARS 12,000 each. Long treatments, such as orthodontics or implants, are quoted after a consultation with the dentist.

## Which services do you offer?

General dentistry, cleaning, whitening, cavity fillings, orthodontics, implants and children's dentistry. We do not perform surgery under general anesthesia.
