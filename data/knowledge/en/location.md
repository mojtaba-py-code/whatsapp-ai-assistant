# Location and directions

## Where are you located? (address)

We are at Avenida Ejemplo 1234, Palermo, Buenos Aires City. This is a fictional demo address.

## How do I get there?

- By subway (subte): three blocks from the nearest station of line D.
- By bus: several lines stop less than a block away.
- By car: there is a paid parking lot half a block away; the clinic has no parking of its own.

## Accessibility

The clinic is on the ground floor and is wheelchair accessible.
