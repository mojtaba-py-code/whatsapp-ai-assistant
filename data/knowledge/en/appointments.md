# Appointments and cancellations

## How do I book an appointment?

Type "appointment" in this chat. The assistant records your request and the team confirms it on WhatsApp during opening hours. A request is not a confirmed appointment until you receive that confirmation.

## How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

If you cannot come, let us know at least 24 hours in advance by replying in this chat. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice and no-shows have a fee of ARS 10,000 (example amount).

## Punctuality

Please arrive 10 minutes early. If you are more than 15 minutes late, the appointment may need to be rescheduled.
